Job-Search OS

The workflow, and the human gates that hold it.

The seven stages

RECEIVE

Leads come in from job boards, LinkedIn, and email alerts. The intake is designed so nothing disappears silently.

ORGANIZE

Each lead becomes one structured record: role, employer, location, schedule, pay, requirements, deadline, source. Unknown fields stay unknown — never guessed, never backfilled.

ANALYZE

Facts are organized into a clear brief. Fit and concerns are your judgment — the system does not rank candidates or tell you what to pursue.

DRAFT

Application materials and response drafts are prepared for your review, with exact proposed text.

HUMAN REVIEW

Every draft and every suggested action waits for your explicit decision.

HUMAN ACTION

You, and only you, submit applications, send responses, and follow up — in your own accounts. The system never acts in your name.

RECORD RESULT

Outcomes return to the register with dates, deciders, and sources.

The seven gates

No work proceeds past an open gate:

  1. Whether to pursue an opportunity.
  2. Whether to apply.
  3. Whether to respond to a recruiter.
  4. What to communicate (exact text approved before sending).
  5. Whether to accept an interview.
  6. Whether to proceed after an interview.
  7. Whether to accept an offer.

Preserved on this page

PREPARATION IS NOT PERMISSION. The system prepares; the human decides. It does not make employment decisions and does not submit applications automatically.
