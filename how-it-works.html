How It Works

A schematic walkthrough of one intended engagement week.

Stated on this page

This describes the intended service week. No customer week has been run. The shape comes from the documented method, not from a delivered engagement.

Day 0 — INTAKE

You share your lead sources, target roles, and where you lose track today.

Day 1 — SETUP

Your Opportunity Register is created — one record per live lead, with sources attached.

Days 2–6 — OPERATE

New leads organized daily. Deadlines flagged. Missing information flagged. Draft packets prepared for your review.

Day 7 — REVIEW

We walk through the register together: decisions made, next actions, what's still unknown.

HANDOFF

The register is yours, with operating notes so you can continue alone.
