Services

One service, defined precisely.

One-week operated job-search organization

Defined, never delivered

What's included:

  • Setup of your Opportunity Register from your existing lead sources.
  • Daily organizing for one week.
  • Deadline and missing-information flags.
  • Draft packets for your review.
  • End-of-week review session.
  • Handoff with operating notes.

What it is not

  • Not a staffing agency.
  • Not automated applying.
  • No submissions or messages sent on your behalf.
  • No contact with recruiters or employers.
  • No inferred facts.
  • No credential holding.
  • No promised outcomes.

Supporting capabilities

Opportunity research and organization; workflow and obligation consulting — defined but not separately offered.

Pricing

Not established — to be determined only if the service is ever offered. No packages. No tiers.
