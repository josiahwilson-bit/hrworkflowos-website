Job-Search OS — a human-operated system for organizing a high-volume job search

One-week operated job-search organization: a human-run system, not software, not an agency.

Who it is for

Job seekers running high-volume searches who lose track of leads, deadlines, and follow-ups.

The problem it addresses

Leads arrive from everywhere — job boards, LinkedIn, email alerts. Deadlines get missed. Follow-ups fall through. Nobody remembers which version of the resume went where. In a documented review of 8 live leads, every single one required manual transcription into a usable record, and 7 of 8 had spawned a duplicated authorization entry.

How the workflow works

  1. RECEIVE — leads come in; nothing is lost silently.
  2. ORGANIZE — each lead becomes one structured record.
  3. ANALYZE — facts organized; fit is your judgment, not a ranking.
  4. DRAFT — application materials prepared for your review.
  5. HUMAN REVIEW — every draft waits for you.
  6. HUMAN ACTION — you, and only you, apply and respond.
  7. RECORD RESULT — outcomes go back into the register.

What you keep control over

Every decision. The system prepares; you decide. Seven explicit decision gates — pursue, apply, respond, exact message text, interview, post-interview, offer — and no work proceeds past an open gate.

Documented evidence

In the documented 12-item backlog trial, 12 records were captured in a single pass each, with 0 duplicate fields, 0 status errors, and 44 unknown fields across 12 records. The trial recorded zero inference and preserved unknown where information was absent.

Boundary, stated plainly

This is a method, demonstrated on a real job search. It is not market validation, and we claim no customer results. Demonstrated is not validated demand.

Service boundary

Available now: none — the service is intended only, not yet offered. In the future: an inquiry path, when authorized.
